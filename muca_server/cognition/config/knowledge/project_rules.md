# Project rules
Type: normative · Version: v1 (2026-10-07) · Source: AUI 2026/27 Lesson 1 (Course Introduction), slides 43–44 and 51

## Project topics and assignment (slide 43)
- Project specifications are provided by the teaching team, as a catalogue of many project topics.
- Students create their own group.
- Each group selects 3 preferred projects, ranked by priority.
- The teaching team assigns a project to each group among its preferred ones, trying to respect the ranking.

## Team composition (slide 43)
- Teams are multidisciplinary: AT LEAST one designer and one engineer per team. "Designers-only" groups are not accepted.
- Exchange students should organize their project work and timing to avoid extra stress in the group.

## Contribution and attendance (slide 44)
- All group members must contribute equally (in terms of effort) to the project work.
- All group members must be present at each tutoring session; attendance is tracked.
- If the teaching team or the students feel participation is unbalanced during the course, a meeting is organized to clarify the issue.
- If participation is unbalanced at the end of the course, a meeting is organized and different group members might receive different scores.

## Typical students' questions (slide 51)
- **"Can we propose our own project?"** In general, no. Good ideas can be discussed, but they must be related to the main interaction paradigms of the course, and the teaching team probably does not have enough people to tutor them.
- **"Can I do the project alone?"** In general, no. Teamwork is one of the skills the course wants students to practise, and the projects are too complex for a single student. Students with major-force reasons (health, family issues…) that make group work very difficult should contact the teaching team to discuss how to manage the situation.
